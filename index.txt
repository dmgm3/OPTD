About the studio
Option D Films is a small independent production company making character-driven shorts and features on modest budgets and real locations. We shoot fast, cut close to the bone, and care more about a true moment than a polished one.
Every project on this page was shot with a crew of five or fewer. If that's the kind of collaborator you're looking for, get in touch below.
